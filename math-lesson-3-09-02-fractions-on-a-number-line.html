Essential Question

Where does a fraction live on a number line?

A fraction is a number. It has its own spot on the number line.

Today's Objectives

You will…

  • Cut the line from 0 to 1 into equal parts with tick marks.
  • Count jumps from 0 to find a fraction, then label it.
  • Tell if a fraction is closer to 0 or to 1, and keep counting past 1.

🎬 Draw It, Cut It, Count It
Learn

Draw it, cut it, count it, label it

  1. Draw a line. Put 0 at the start and 1 at the end.
  2. Cut it into equal parts. For 7 parts, draw 6 tick marks. Count the spaces, not the marks.
  3. Count jumps from 0. Each jump is a unit fraction: one-seventh.
  4. Label the spot. 4 jumps is 47. 7 jumps lands on 1. The line keeps going: 8 jumps is 87.
The Math Crew

Four ways to see the line in sevenths

6 tick marks make 7 equal spaces.
Count the jumps: one-seventh, two-sevenths, three-sevenths, four-sevenths.
77 lands right on 1.
One more jump is 87. That's past 1.
