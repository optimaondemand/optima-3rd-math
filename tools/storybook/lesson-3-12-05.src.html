🎬 Two Names for Every Triangle
Learn

Names for the sides

Tick marks show which sides are the same length: sides with the same marks match. All 3 sides the same: equilateral. At least 2 sides the same: isosceles. No sides the same: scalene.

An equilateral triangle has at least 2 equal sides, too, so it is also isosceles. When we sort, we use the name that tells the most: equilateral.

Learn

Names for the angles

Check every corner with a set square. All 3 angles smaller than a square corner: acute. One square corner: right triangle. One angle bigger than a square corner: obtuse.

Learn

Two names for one triangle

Every triangle gets two names. Count the equal sides for one name. Check the corners for the other. This one is isosceles and right. Size doesn't change the names.

The Math Crew

Four ways to stay on track

I count tick marks first. Matching marks mean matching sides.
I check all 3 corners, not just one. One wide corner makes it obtuse.
I always give two names: one for the sides, one for the angles.
Big or tiny, a triangle keeps its names. I look at the corners, not the size.
